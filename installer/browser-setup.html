Add ADM to your browser

ADM catches downloads and videos through a small browser extension that comes with it. Until the extension is in the browsers' stores, it is added by hand, once. It takes about a minute.

  1. Copy this, paste it into Chrome's address bar, and press Enter. (Browsers don't let a page open it for you.)
    chrome://extensions
  2. Turn on Developer mode, at the top right.
  3. Click Load unpacked. In the window that opens, paste this folder into the Folder box at the bottom, then click Select Folder.
  4. ADM's icon is under the puzzle piece at the right of the toolbar. Click the pin beside it to keep it in view.

After updating ADM, open chrome://extensions again and click ADM's reload button (↻).

If Chrome ever offers to turn off developer-mode extensions, keep ADM's on, or downloads go back to the browser.

  1. Copy this, paste it into Edge's address bar, and press Enter. (Browsers don't let a page open it for you.)
    edge://extensions
  2. Turn on Developer mode, in the list on the left (on a narrow window, open it with ☰).
  3. Click Load unpacked. In the window that opens, paste this folder into the Folder box at the bottom, then click Select Folder.
  4. To keep ADM's icon in view, click the Extensions button (the puzzle piece) in the toolbar, then the eye beside ADM.

After updating ADM, open edge://extensions again and click ADM's Reload.

If Edge, when it starts, offers to turn off extensions in developer mode, keep ADM's on, or downloads go back to the browser.

  1. Copy this, paste it into Firefox's address bar, and press Enter. (Browsers don't let a page open it for you.)
    about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox
  2. Click Load Temporary Add-on…. In the window that opens, paste this into the File name box, then click Open.

Firefox removes an add-on loaded this way when it closes, so do this again after each restart of Firefox, until ADM is in Firefox's add-on store. Firefox 128 or later is needed.

This page is in ADM's folder as Browser setup.html, and in the Start menu as Add ADM to your browser.
