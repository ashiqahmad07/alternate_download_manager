ADM's installer also carries three programs that ADM runs to read a site's video and to join
its sound to it. Setup puts them in %LOCALAPPDATA%\ADM\tools, where ADM would otherwise fetch
them on first use. Each is a separate program under its own licence; licenses\video-tools.txt,
beside ADM.exe, names the versions carried.

  yt-dlp  The Unlicense (public domain).  https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp
          Its Windows program carries Python and the libraries yt-dlp uses, each under its
          own licence, as its README lists.
  Deno    The MIT licence, below.  https://github.com/denoland/deno
  FFmpeg  The GNU Lesser General Public License, whose text is in licenses\ffmpeg-LICENSE.txt.
          The build is BtbN's LGPL build, https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds, from FFmpeg's
          source at https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg, at the commit its version names.
